# Phase 3 ARM64 evidence

Date: 2026-08-12. Host: Apple M5, arm64, macOS 26.5.1. Compiler: Apple
Clang 21.0.0. Build: Release `-O3 -DNDEBUG`, `-ffp-contract=off`, native
tuning off. Source tree: `a9c1408` plus the uncommitted Phase 3 evidence and
integrity closure changes. Corpus generated by
`tests/generate_encoder_corpus.py`.

## Phase A CPU shares

Workload: deterministic `long_60s_44100.wav`, scalar psychoacoustics, one
warm-up plus five measured runs. Process CPU-time counters were enabled only
in the profile build.

| quality | total libmpcpsy / encoder | FFT / encoder | FFT / libmpcpsy | PowSpec/window / libmpcpsy | other psy / libmpcpsy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| q5 | 77.42% | 31.39% | 40.66% | 25.27% | 34.11% |
| **q6** | **76.93%** | **31.18%** | **40.59%** | **25.33%** | **34.09%** |
| q7 | 75.38% | 30.71% | 40.67% | 25.41% | 33.75% |

The agreed decision used **FFT as a share of total encoder CPU time**. FFT is
below 35% at every quality. Result: **the `>=35%` FFT decision rule is not
satisfied on ARM64.** FFT does exceed 35% inside `libmpcpsy`, but that is a
different denominator and is not substituted for the agreed rule.

macOS `sample` corroborated the counter profile. q5/q6/q7 scalar profiles
reported 28/33/32 top-of-stack `pow` samples plus 5 `log10` samples from
134/1498/1549 total samples respectively; q5 was contaminated by 129 startup
samples, so its libm ratio is not used quantitatively. q6 and q7 place visible
libm at approximately 2.5% and 2.4% of encoder samples. Compiler-inlined math
is not visible and these are lower-bound sampling estimates.

## End-to-end psychoacoustic A/B

Aggregate of the three long deterministic tracks (30 s 44.1 kHz, 60 s
44.1 kHz, 30 s 48 kHz), median of five encodes per track. The analyser is
AUTO in both arms; only `--psy-impl` changes. Every scalar/SIMD MPC pair was
byte-identical.

| quality | scalar wall | SIMD wall | scalar realtime | SIMD realtime | speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| q5 | 147.55 ms | 134.15 ms | 813.3x | 894.5x | 9.99% |
| **q6** | **148.72 ms** | **135.55 ms** | **806.9x** | **885.3x** | **9.72%** |
| q7 | 152.22 ms | 139.57 ms | 788.3x | 859.8x | 9.06% |

## Isolated spectrum/FFT production mix

One thousand iterations; production q5/q6/q7 call mix. This includes the
retained windowing, FFT, power, phase, packing, and splitting kernels.

| quality | scalar | SIMD | throughput gain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| q5 | 40.481 ms | 26.099 ms | 1.55x |
| **q6** | **41.047 ms** | **25.385 ms** | **1.62x** |
| q7 | 38.838 ms | 26.001 ms | 1.49x |

The explicit lane-parallel FFT materially contributes to the measured
end-to-end gain, but the original total-encoder 35% admission threshold was
not met.
